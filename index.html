🟢 Present Tense

Simple Present

Formula: Subject + V1 + Object

Example: I play football.

Present Continuous

Formula: Subject + am/is/are + V-ing

Example: I am playing football.

Present Perfect

Formula: Subject + has/have + V3

Example: I have played football.

Present Perfect Continuous

Formula: Subject + has/have been + V-ing

Example: I have been playing football.

🔵 Past Tense

Simple Past

Formula: Subject + V2 + Object

Example: I played football.

Past Continuous

Formula: Subject + was/were + V-ing

Example: I was playing football.

Past Perfect

Formula: Subject + had + V3

Example: I had played football.

Past Perfect Continuous

Formula: Subject + had been + V-ing

Example: I had been playing football.

🟣 Future Tense

Simple Future

Formula: Subject + will + V1

Example: I will play football.

Future Continuous

Formula: Subject + will be + V-ing

Example: I will be playing football.

Future Perfect

Formula: Subject + will have + V3

Example: I will have played football.

Future Perfect Continuous

Formula: Subject + will have been + V-ing

Example: I will have been playing football.
