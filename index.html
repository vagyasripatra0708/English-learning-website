GRAMMAR FOUNDATION

📝 English Grammar

Understand the basic building blocks of correct English.

01

Noun

A noun names a person, place, animal, thing or idea.

Rahul, Kolkata, dog, book, honesty
02

Pronoun

A pronoun is used instead of a noun.

Rahul is intelligent. He studies daily.
03

Verb

A verb shows an action or state.

run, eat, write, study, sleep
04

Adjective

An adjective describes a noun or pronoun.

She has a beautiful dress.
05

Adverb

An adverb describes a verb, adjective or another adverb.

He runs quickly.
06

Preposition

A preposition shows a relationship between words.

in, on, at, under, beside
SENTENCE FORMULA

Subject + Verb + Object

Rahul → plays → football

VOICE MASTERCLASS

🔄 Active & Passive Voice

Learn how sentence focus changes.

🟢

Active Voice

The subject performs the action.

Ram writes a letter.
Ram = Subject
writes = Verb
letter = Object
🔵

Passive Voice

The receiver of the action becomes the focus.

A letter is written by Ram.
letter = New Subject
is written = Passive Verb
Ram = Doer
BASIC FORMULA

Object + Helping Verb + V3 + by + Subject

ACTIVE Ram writes a letter.
→
PASSIVE A letter is written by Ram.
TENSE MASTERCLASS

⏰ 12 English Tenses

Learn the basic formula and example of every tense.

1. Present Simple

Subject + V1

I play football.

2. Present Continuous

Subject + is/am/are + V-ing

I am playing football.

3. Present Perfect

Subject + has/have + V3

I have played football.

4. Present Perfect Continuous

has/have been + V-ing

I have been playing.

5. Past Simple

Subject + V2

I played football.

6. Past Continuous

was/were + V-ing

I was playing.

7. Past Perfect

had + V3

I had played.

8. Past Perfect Continuous

had been + V-ing

I had been playing.

9. Future Simple

will + V1

I will play.

10. Future Continuous

will be + V-ing

I will be playing.

11. Future Perfect

will have + V3

I will have played.

12. Future Perfect Continuous

will have been + V-ing

I will have been playing.
